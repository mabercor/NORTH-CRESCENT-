Frequently Asked Questions About Janitorial Services in Moncton

How much do janitorial services cost for an office in Moncton?

Janitorial service costs depend on facility size, cleaning frequency, occupancy levels, and operational requirements. North Crescent Facility Solutions provides customized maintenance programs for offices, clinics, retail spaces, and commercial facilities throughout Greater Moncton.

How often should a commercial facility be professionally cleaned?

Cleaning frequency depends on foot traffic, industry requirements, employee count, and facility usage. Most commercial facilities benefit from recurring cleaning schedules that maintain consistent cleanliness, safety, and professional presentation.

Can janitorial services be scheduled after business hours?

Yes. We offer flexible janitorial schedules including evenings, overnight service, weekends, and off-peak hours to minimize disruption to your daily operations.

What is included in a commercial janitorial program?

A structured janitorial program may include routine cleaning, restroom sanitation, waste removal, floor care, high-touch disinfection, supply monitoring, inspections, and scheduled deep cleaning based on facility requirements.

How do I know if my current cleaning company is doing a good job?

A professional cleaning provider should deliver consistent results, follow documented procedures, conduct regular inspections, and maintain clear quality standards. If cleaning quality varies from visit to visit, the process may lack proper operational controls.

What is the difference between janitorial services and commercial cleaning?

Commercial cleaning often refers to individual cleaning projects, while janitorial services focus on recurring facility maintenance programs designed to keep environments consistently clean, organized, and operational over time.
